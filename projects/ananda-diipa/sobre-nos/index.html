About Ananda Diipa

Who We Are

A Community,
a Forest,
a Practice

Ananda Diipa was founded by Didi Ananda Parvati and Dada Rainjitananda, both long-time teachers in the Ananda Marga tradition. After years of teaching in ashrams across Europe and India, they found this land in the Black Forest and recognised it immediately: this is the place.

The name "Ananda Diipa" means "island of bliss" in Sanskrit. It reflects the intention behind everything here: to create a genuine refuge, not a wellness commodity. A place where ancient practices are taught with integrity, and where the forest itself is honoured as a co-teacher.

Today the centre hosts over 200 retreat participants per year, trains teachers from across Europe, and maintains a small resident community that tends the land and the practice year-round.

Our Journey

From a Vision in
the Forest to a Living Centre

The Land is Found

Didi and Dada discover a 25-hectare property in the Black Forest near Freiburg. The old farmhouse has been abandoned for 12 years. They see not the ruin, but the possibility. The first meditation is held under the trees before any renovation begins.

2014
2016

The Doors Open

After two years of renovation using local craftspeople and natural materials, Ananda Diipa opens its first retreat. Eight participants. One week. By the end of the week, two of them have signed up to stay on as volunteers.

The Shala is Built

A purpose-built yoga shala is completed using locally sourced timber. The building is designed to disappear into the forest canopy when seen from above. It is heated in winter by a wood-burning system and cooled in summer by the forest itself.

2019
2024

Teacher Training Accredited

Our 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training receives Yoga Alliance accreditation. The programme is now delivered twice yearly with a waiting list. We remain deliberately small: depth matters more than scale.

What Guides Us

Our Values

Integrity of Practice

We teach from lineage, not trend. The practices we offer are rooted in the Tantra yoga tradition and transmitted with rigour and care. We do not dilute to make things easier to sell.

Ecological Responsibility

The forest is not a backdrop. It is the reason we are here. We manage the land using permaculture principles, source food from local organic farms, and heat with wood from our own sustainable harvest.

Genuine Welcome

Every guest is received as they are. We do not ask you to perform wellness or demonstrate commitment. Come tired, come uncertain. You are welcome exactly as you arrive.

Depth Over Speed

We limit group sizes deliberately. We offer fewer programmes than we could. We believe transformation needs time, repetition, and silence. We protect those conditions above all else.
