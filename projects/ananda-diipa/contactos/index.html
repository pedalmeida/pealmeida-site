FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before the retreat start date. Cancellations within 14 days are charged 50% of the total. If we can fill your place from the waiting list, we refund in full.

Are meals included?

Yes. All programmes include three daily plant-based meals prepared from organic, locally sourced ingredients. We cater for all dietary requirements, please let us know when you apply.

Do I need prior experience in yoga or meditation?

No experience is required for weekend and week-long retreats. The 200-hour teacher training requires at least one year of consistent personal practice.

What is the accommodation like?

We offer simple, comfortable private and shared rooms in the main house and two smaller forest cabins. Bedding and towels are provided. Rooms are intentionally simple to support the retreat atmosphere.

How do I get there?

We are 40 minutes from Freiburg im Breisgau by car. The nearest train station is Kirchzarten (20 minutes by taxi). We can arrange shared transfers from Freiburg Hbf on retreat start and end days - ask us when booking.

Is Wi-Fi available?

We intentionally keep Ananda Diipa a low-connectivity environment. There is no Wi-Fi in the guest rooms or shala. A single terminal is available in the reception area for essential communications.
