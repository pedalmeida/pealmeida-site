Welcome

A Place to Arrive
in Yourself

Ananda Diipa means "island of bliss" in Sanskrit. We chose this name because that is what this place is: a refuge from the noise of modern life, where ancient practices and the healing power of nature work together.

Founded in 2016 by teachers trained in the Ananda Marga tradition, the centre offers residential retreats, teacher training, and open programmes for anyone who feels the call to go deeper. No experience necessary. Only an open heart.

The Land

Where Forest
Becomes Teacher

The Black Forest is one of Europe's most ancient and biodiverse landscapes. Our 25-hectare property sits at 800 metres, surrounded by centuries-old silver fir, mountain streams, and meadows that bloom from April through October.

Freiburg im Breisgau, one of Germany's sunniest cities and a hub of sustainable living, is 40 minutes away. The forest, however, is already here. You arrived the moment you stepped through the gate.
