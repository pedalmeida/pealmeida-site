📍 What the data shows

Three signals we have to hear before we scale pracar.

Across the quantitative and qualitative findings, three patterns recur with enough force that they should shape any AK outreach decision.

01 · The entry door

They come for pain, not for God

28.5% start to escape a hard state. 29.9% to find meaning. Only 7.3% to "feel God". The 140-person newcomer study confirms anxiety and fear are the #1 barrier to continued practice. The entry door is coping, meaning, calm.

02 · The fragile promise

Warmth sold, theatre delivered

61.5% are drawn by the warm atmosphere; 32.2% are blocked by poor interpersonal interaction once inside. Newcomers describe acharyas using cold, directive tone; long-time members describe "hypocrisy" and "everyone tries to look better than they are". The strongest promise becomes the biggest source of detractors.

03 · The leak after initiation

A vacuum in the first 90 days

Less than 20% of yearly-course participants get initiated. Of those who do, multiple describe the post-initiation period as silence: no curator, no clear next step, no one to ask. The funnel converts, the retention leaks.

🛠️ What Maetreya's team is building

A four-stage funnel. Retreats at the end, not the start.

BMTR, the AM Global Team's marketing working group led by Maetreya, has converged on a single playbook. Each stage has measurable conversion. Proof points already exist in Germany (100+ per retreat with problem-targeted ads) and Russia (24/day join the bot, 80/day apply for the course).

Stage 1 · Cold
Content + targeted ads

Address a named problem

Insomnia, panic attacks, work stress. Not generic "spirituality". Target: 100M monthly views across 100+ social accounts.

Stage 2 · Warm
Meditation marathon

Free or low-cost lead magnet

Pre-recorded acharya videos, adaptable per region/language. Two versions: self-paced free, or paid cohort with facilitator. Cohort converts materially better.

Stage 3 · Engaged
Comprehensive AM course

Meditation + yoga + philosophy + neohumanism

All in one programme. Still in scripting; AM's equivalent of Inner Engineering does not exist yet.

Stage 4 · Committed
Retreat (at the end)

Only after trust is built

Cold retreat attendees were the leakiest part of the old funnel. Germany shows the new shape works: 100+ per retreat.

✅ What we copy first

Three moves. Cheap. Immediate.

From the eight interventions BMTR has published, three map directly onto AK's current stage. Small enough to start this quarter, structural enough to compound. They feed into Maetreya's funnel from the local end.

01 · Mentorship

The first 90 days, designed

A public list of 3 to 5 senior PT margis available to answer. Opt-in, not assigned. Before we initiate newcomers in volume.

02 · Entry course

"What is yoga" in Portuguese

Introductory course based on the Eight Limbs. Adult language. Positions AM inside the yogic tradition, against the "is AM a sect?" confusion.

03 · Hub

One Portuguese page that exists

It does not need to be beautiful. It needs to exist. What AM is, who teaches, contacts, how to start. Today a Portuguese-speaking seeker finds nothing structured.
