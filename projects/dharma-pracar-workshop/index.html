🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 1 of 6

🌫️ Unaware

Has never heard of AM. It does not exist in their world. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 1
Pain points
  • Targeting "everyone 18-65" with no defined ICP. The right person never sees us.
  • No Portuguese content factory. The Portuguese-speaking seeker who would resonate finds nothing structured.
  • Generic "spirituality" ad copy. Doesn't name a problem anyone is actively searching for.
  • "Is AM a sect?" confusion in cold audience, no public-facing answer in PT.
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • ICP-first ads. One persona, one pain ("anxiety in 30s women PT"). Test one ad. Generic targeting is the failure.
  • Problem-targeted creative. Insomnia, panic attacks, work stress, burnout. Germany model: 100+ per retreat with this exact shift.
  • AM Content Fabric (project #03). Plug into global network of 10+ accounts, target 100M monthly views → 10-20k people/month into the funnel.
  • AM Retreat Marketing (project #01). Central marketing team (9 people) already live in Sweden, Germany, Italy. Apply the playbook to PT.
🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 2 of 6

🌱 First Contact

Hears about AM. A friend, an ad, a video, a book. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 2
Pain points
  • We talk philosophy. They arrive in pain. 60% of entrants are in anxiety/fear, 28.5% to escape a hard state, 29.9% to find meaning, only 7.3% to "feel God".
  • Warmth is what's sold (61.5% drawn by the warm atmosphere) but 32.2% report poor interpersonal interaction once inside.
  • Newcomers describe acharya tone as cold and directive.
  • No single PT page that explains what AM is, who teaches, how to start, contacts.
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • Lead with the named outcome (sleep, calm, focus, relief) before the philosophy. Transcendence is what we offer after they walk in.
  • AM Meditation Marathons (project #11). Russia version passed by 10,000+. 24/day join the bot, 80/day apply for the course.
  • "What is Yoga" entry course in PT. Based on the Eight Limbs. Adult language. Positions AM inside the yogic tradition.
  • One PT hub page that exists. What AM is, who teaches, how to start, contacts. Needs to exist before it needs to be beautiful.
  • AM Social Media (project #02). Instagram verified. Expand to TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube.
🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 3 of 6

🚪 First Experience

Shows up. Yoga class, kiirtan, retreat, workshop. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 3
Pain points
  • Retreats pushed too early in the journey. Cold retreat attendees were the leakiest part of the old funnel.
  • Only people who already meditated or watched AM YouTube before the retreat actually stayed.
  • Long-time members report "hypocrisy" and "everyone tries to look better than they are". The strongest promise (warm community) becomes the biggest source of detractors.
  • No low-commitment first step between "saw an ad" and "show up to a retreat".
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • AM App (project #06). Pre-retreat learning app, newcomers learn meditation and philosophy before being invited to retreat. 3 specialists, MVP soon, 10,000 learners/month target.
  • AM Online (project #09). Live online classes where acharyas teach philosophy and practices. In approval, launching soon.
  • Paid cohort marathon with facilitator + homework. Materially higher conversion than self-paced version.
  • Retreat moves to the end of the funnel, not the start. Germany shows the new shape works: 100+ per retreat once the order is right.
🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 4 of 6

🔁 Returning

Comes back. Familiarity builds. Community starts to feel theirs. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 4
Pain points
  • Anxiety and fear are the #1 barrier to continued practice (140-person newcomer study).
  • Nobody follows up. No mentor, no group, no easy next step after the first experience.
  • Warmth was promised on entry. Theatre delivered inside. The 32% who report poor interpersonal interaction drop here.
  • Senior lay margis are invisible. People who work, have families, speak plain adult language are missing. Monastic-only model leaves this seat empty.
  • No defined first 90 days. No assigned mentor, no peer cohort, no small service tasks from week 1.
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • AM Mentorship (project #10). Small groups of ~10. ISKCON-inspired cell-division model (bhakti vriksha). 100+ people already, Nirmal Krishna leads. Compounds without paid staff.
  • Post-initiation 90 days, designed. Assigned mentor + peer cohort + small defined service tasks from week 1.
  • Paid cohort marathon with facilitator + homework. Builds first accountability loop.
  • Family teachers visible. Hakimov / Torsunov model: married, working teachers who explain practice in adult language.
  • AM CRM (project #14). Kommo handles ads today. Searching for a second platform for the broader margi community.
🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 5 of 6

🌼 Initiation

Receives a personal mantra. Begins a daily sadhana. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 5
Pain points
  • <20% of yearly course participants get initiated. 20-30 start, ~20 finish, only 2-5 initiate. The leak is here.
  • We don't bridge "course done" to "ready for mantra". No clear ladder.
  • AM has no equivalent to Sadhguru's Inner Engineering, the entry course that gives the whole picture in one programme.
  • "If AM disappeared, where would you go?" 39% choose Buddhism. 3x bigger than any other alternative. We are not positioning against the real competitor.
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • AM Flagman Product (project #07). Sadhguru Inner Engineering equivalent: meditation + yoga + philosophy + neohumanism in one entry course. Currently scripting, 2 people. This is the bridge.
  • AM Academy (project #08). Peterson Academy-inspired platform: structured courses on Prout, neohumanism, Yama / Niyama, 16 points. For newcomers and margi. 3 people, looking for team leader.
  • Position against Buddhism specifically. Our advantage: devotion + service + mission + collective structure. Make our edge visible.
  • Lead with ethics. 91% of margis cite "moral reference points" as AM's strongest asset. In a cynical PT moment, this is a real differentiator.
🗺️ The Margi Journey · Stage 6 of 6

🪷 Committed

Sustained practice. Involved in community. May teach or serve. Pain points and possible solutions from Dada Maetreya's research and the 16 global projects.

Stage 6
Pain points
  • Post-initiation period is silence. No curator. No clear next step. The funnel converts, retention leaks.
  • Practice fades without a group. Life clash, no peer cohort, no mentor, no defined service role.
  • No clear ladder from practitioner → volunteer → teacher locally.
  • CSI exposes the outer infrastructure gap: family life 37%, public image 34%, employment fit 36%. Strong on the inside, fragile on the outside.
  • Number of acharyas is shrinking globally. PT has no recruitment pipeline.
Possible solutions · From Maetreya's 16 projects
  • Cell-division mentorship at scale. When one margi finishes a stage, they lead the next group. Proven in Russia. Compounds without paid staff.
  • Become A Monk (project #13). Direct ads + recruitment retreats. 2 retreats done in Sweden, 1 candidate; 2 more planned; sisters' programme in June.
  • AM News (project #12). Weekly newsletter + queryable map of every master unit, jagrti, office, project. 350 → 1,000 subs target. 5 people.
  • AM Business (project #15). Self-sustainability and revenue infrastructure. $5k/month now, $20k target.
  • AM Store (project #16). Worldwide shop, blocked on legal-entity country choice.
Section 3 · 30 minutes

Where does it hurt?

Individual brainwriting. Silent. Honest. One pain per sticky.

01

✍️ Two minutes per stage.

I read the stage out loud. You write what hurts. As many stickies as you like.

02

🤫 Silence is the discipline.

No discussion while we write. Kiirtan plays low. The wall is the conversation.

03

📌 Slap it on the column.

One column per stage. After each round, walk up, post, sit down.

Section 3 · still 30 minutes

Now we go stage by stage, out loud.

Once the wall is full, Parantapa walks through each stage. Every participant reads their own stickies aloud, in their own words. The room hears the wall.

🗣️ One person at a time 🪷 Listen, do not debate ✨ Every voice in the room
Section 4 · 15 minutes

Cluster, read aloud, 5 dots each.

Kaoverii reads the wall back to us, one cluster at a time. Did we hear this right? Then everyone votes with 5 dots on the pains blocking our growth.

01

🔊 Read each cluster.

Kaoverii names what it is in one phrase. Parantapa asks: did we miss anything?

02

➕ Add what is missing.

New stickies on the fly. Better to surface it now than carry it later.

03

🟢 Five dots each.

Place them on the pain clusters you feel are most blocking. Spend them as you like.

☕ Pause

Break. 10 minutes.

Parantapa and Kaoverii turn the top 3 pains into HMWs while you stretch.
Section 5 · 30 minutes

Ten ideas each, then present.

Same format. Solution stickies, different color. Pick any pain on the wall. Aim for ten ideas each, one per sticky. Volume now, filter later.

01

✍️ Ten minutes, ten ideas.

Silent. Write at least ten solution stickies, one per pain you want to fix. Quantity unlocks quality. Kiirtan plays low.

02

🎤 Twenty minutes round-table.

Each person presents their ten. PA and KV cluster on the fly as ideas land.

03

📚 Read aloud, group.

We end with clean solution clusters on the wall, ready for the vote.
